- Initial complete release of `optimize-content-discoverability`.

- Search-intent engine.

- Semantic-coverage engine.
- Entity recognition and terminology-consistency engine.
- E-E-A-T and trust engine with explicit prohibition on invented credentials.
- AI-search and retrieval-readiness engine.
- Google AI Overview readiness assessment.
- Featured-snippet candidate generation.
- Google Discover readiness assessment.
- Knowledge-graph mapping.
- Schema recommendation engine.
- Internal-linking engine with no invented URLs.
- Image and visual metadata package.
- Content-structure and readability engine.
- Competitor-intelligence engine activated only with current, traceable evidence.
- Topical-authority engine.
- Content-memory and cannibalization engine.
- Content-decay and refresh engine.
- Editorial-distribution intelligence.
- Complete headline, metadata, FAQ and lifecycle packages.
- Diagnostic scoring model with `NOT_ASSESSED` handling.
- Ten mandatory quality gates.
- Failure taxonomy `DISC-001` through `DISC-020`.
- Maximum of two automatic revision attempts.
- Normalized `DiscoverabilityPackage` YAML contract.
- Human-readable output order for the future MacroAlloc Article Package.
- Explicit handoff contract to `review-article`.

### Changed

- Expanded scope beyond conventional SEO to search, AI retrieval, topical authority, content memory, lifecycle and controlled distribution.
- Replaced rigid keyword-density logic with natural-language and intent-based review.
- Replaced unsupported ranking promises with calibrated readiness assessments.
- Separated optional advanced modules from mandatory execution so missing competitor or analytics data does not block basic optimization.

### Safeguards

- No factual, numerical, causal, analytical or compliance change is permitted without re-verification.
- No fabricated keyword volume, difficulty, ranking, traffic, competitor, schema or content-inventory claims.
- No publication approval or CMS action.
- No invented author credentials, endorsements or first-hand experience.
- No third automatic revision.

## 1.1.0 — US-first selection preservation

- Requires `TOPIC_SELECTED` lineage and explicit human-selection evidence.
- Preserves the selected topic and locked angle.
- Makes US-English metadata for the US priority market mandatory.
- Keeps secondary-language SEO outside the primary package.
