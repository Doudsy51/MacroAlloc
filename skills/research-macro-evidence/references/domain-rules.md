# Research Macro Evidence — Domain Rules

## Source hierarchy

Prefer primary official sources: statistical agencies, central banks, regulators, government publications, company filings, fund issuers, exchanges, and original datasets. Use high-quality secondary sources for context and competing interpretations. Record interested-party incentives and never substitute search-result snippets for source review.

## Evidence handling

- Distinguish event time, publication time, revision time, and retrieval time.
- Preserve units, seasonal adjustment, nominal/real basis, frequency, geography, and comparison period.
- Record corrections, revisions, base effects, temporary components, and methodological breaks.
- Paraphrase accurately and keep quotations short, exact, attributable, and source-linked.
- Mark unavailable or unverified planned evidence explicitly; never promote it to confirmed evidence.

## Causal and market reasoning

- Separate observed co-movement from supported causation.
- Decompose every central causal pivot into premises and transmission links.
- Record credible alternative explanations and what evidence would distinguish them.
- Do not infer a market move's cause from timing alone.
- Separate economic outcomes, market expectations, valuation effects, and portfolio implications.

## Language and scope

Produce the canonical dossier in US English. A requested translation is a separate downstream artifact and never replaces the primary dossier. Preserve the human-selected topic and angle; escalate evidence that materially contradicts the angle instead of silently changing it.
