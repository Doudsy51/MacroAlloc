# Research Macro Evidence — Quality and Tests

## Contents

- Hard gates
- Minimum evaluations
- Completion criteria

## Hard gates

All must pass for `EVIDENCE_DOSSIER_READY_FOR_WRITING`:

1. Selection lineage is complete and matches the active shortlist.
2. Locked topic, angle, language, and publication window are preserved.
3. Every material planned claim has traceable support or is prohibited.
4. Source records include identity, date, retrieval evidence, scope, and limitations.
5. Material data are normalized and revisions or base effects are addressed.
6. Causal pivots and credible alternatives are documented.
7. Evidence is fresh enough for the planned publication window.
8. The writer can distinguish facts, attribution, uncertainty, interpretation, and scenarios.

Scores never override a failed hard gate.

## Minimum evaluations

- Direct selected-topic research request.
- Indirect request to prepare evidence before writing.
- Missing selection evidence.
- Request to write the article instead of research.
- Mandatory official source unavailable.
- Evidence contradicts the locked angle.
- Attempt to invent unavailable current data.
- Complete dossier handoff to the writer.

## Completion criteria

Return success only with an internally consistent lineage object, evidence dossier, source register, question coverage, gates, issues, and writer handoff. Keep the status `TESTING` until fresh-context integration tests pass.
